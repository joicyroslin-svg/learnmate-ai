# LearnMate AI

## AI-Powered Personalized Study Planning System

LearnMate AI is a proposed AI-powered personalized study planning system designed to help students organize their academic activities according to their individual needs, priorities, and available study time.

The project is being developed as part of the **Virtual AI Innovation Internship**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Students often have multiple subjects, different examination dates, varying academic strengths and weaknesses, difficult topics, and limited study time. Creating and maintaining an effective study schedule can therefore be challenging.

LearnMate AI aims to address this problem by using student-specific information to generate personalized study recommendations.

The proposed system can consider factors such as:

* Subjects being studied
* Examination dates
* Available study hours
* Previous academic performance
* Topic difficulty
* Study preferences
* Study progress

Based on these factors, the system will recommend study priorities and generate a personalized study plan.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Traditional fixed study timetables may not adequately consider the individual needs of different students.

Two students studying the same subjects may have different:

* Academic strengths
* Academic weaknesses
* Available study time
* Examination schedules
* Topic difficulties
* Learning progress

Therefore, there is a need for a more personalized and adaptable approach to study planning.

LearnMate AI proposes to address this challenge through AI-based recommendations that can adapt to changes in student progress and circumstances.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

LearnMate AI will analyze relevant student information and generate personalized study recommendations.

The proposed workflow is:

**Student Information → Data Processing → Student Profile → Priority Analysis → AI Recommendation → Personalized Study Plan → Progress Tracking → Updated Recommendations**

The system is intended to act as a supportive planning assistant while allowing students to remain in control of their final study decisions.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### Personalized Study Planning

Generate study plans based on individual student requirements and available study time.

### Subject and Topic Prioritization

Identify subjects and topics that may require greater attention based on factors such as examination deadlines, difficulty, and previous performance.

### Performance-Based Recommendations

Use academic performance information to help determine study priorities.

### Progress Tracking

Allow students to record completed study activities and update their progress.

### Adaptive Recommendations

Modify future recommendations based on updated student progress and circumstances.

### Examination Deadline Awareness

Consider upcoming examination dates when determining study priorities.

### Student-Controlled Planning

Provide recommendations while allowing students to review and modify the suggested plan.

---

## 🤖 Proposed AI Approach

The initial system is proposed to use a hybrid approach combining:

1. Rule-based prioritization
2. Recommendation techniques
3. Future machine-learning approaches

Priority calculations may consider:

* Examination proximity
* Previous academic performance
* Topic difficulty
* Amount of material remaining
* Available study time
* Current progress

Future development can evaluate machine-learning techniques to determine whether they improve recommendation quality and personalization.
